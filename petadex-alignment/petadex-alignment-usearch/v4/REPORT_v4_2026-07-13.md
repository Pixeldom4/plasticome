## v1-overlay coherence (report, don't enforce)

- **205 of 476** nodes carry a v1 `component`/`cath` overlay (all 205 v1 md5 nodes survived
  the merge into the finalized set).
- **Exactly one** v1 label is split across >1 v4 component: **label 35** → {C001, C033}.
  C033 is the singleton `Enzyme 305 like` (GxsBSedJan11_10251181, 335 aa); the rest of
  label 35 sits inside the big α/β-hydrolase component C001. Expected under a flat merged
  partition, documented not corrected (§7 overlay coherence).
- Most low-numbered v1 families that were their own components in v2/v3 remain **singleton
  components** here (e.g. labels 6, 7, 8, 18, 19, 24, 29, 33, 34, 36–41, 43) — they simply
  have no ≥30%/e<1e−5 neighbor in the 476-node set at the more-conservative 476-db threshold.

## Fragment / substring caveats (G3)

Per decision 3 no fragments were filtered. Traceable consequences:

- **The 28-aa sequence** (P80008.1, *Arylacylamidase*, node m0027) is **not a singleton** —
  it attaches into **C013** (rank 3) via a short high-identity patch. This is exactly the
  G3-flagged short-patch behaviour; the edge is real per the criterion but rests on ~28 aa.
- The **24 singletons** are all full-length sequences (smallest 112 aa, porcine pancreatic
  lipase C036); no substring-contained node became a spurious singleton.
